## Purpose

Build a web-based platform where sellers list unpaid invoices for discounted sale to investors.

## Scope

- Registration and role-based access for sellers, investors and administrators
- Invoice upload and administration
- Browsing, bidding or purchase
- Payment and settlement status tracking
- History and reports

The approved SRS will determine the exact scope.

## Out of scope

- Real fund movement or a live payment provider
- Real debtor or customer financial records; synthetic data is used
- Live operation after the course, unless a plan extension is approved
- AI/ML features
